How the numbers work

For use n, Imperium cost = starting cost + (n − 1) × repeat increase. Total spent = n × starting cost + repeat increase × n × (n − 1) ÷ 2.

For standard rites, the initial affinity threshold unlocks use 1. Each purchase resets that rite’s accumulated affinity; the threshold for the next use is 50 points higher. For example, Expansive Growth needs 160 points to unlock, then 210 points after the first purchase, 260 after the second, and so on. Recharge uses affinity points, so more affinity income means fewer turns.

Expanded Governance unlocks at 100 total affinity and has no recharge thereafter. It and Expansive Growth increase the same city cap but have separate purchase counts and price increases. Increasing the cap through one does not advance the other’s price.
