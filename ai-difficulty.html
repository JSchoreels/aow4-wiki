What makes the AI harder?

Compare battle behavior, economic advantages, and the rules that shape an AI empire. Normal and Hard share a tactical profile; Hard changes the resources and strategic freedom behind it.

More careful in battle

Forgiving gives way to Challenging, which prioritizes kills and uses more careful positioning.

Normal → Hard

More campaign pressure

The same battle profile, with larger army targets, stronger discounts, and earlier permission to declare war.

Hard → Brutal

Stronger troops and economy

Challenging+ adds damage modifiers. Strategic bonuses increase again, and world threat rises to High.

Cheaper upkeep still matters after Manta.

An army with 100 Gold upkeep before difficulty modifiers costs Hard AI 85 and Brutal AI 70. At the same upkeep budget, 30% cheaper upkeep could support about 43% more equivalent troops. Recruitment costs and available resources still constrain that army.

Sources & limits

A comparison you can inspect

  • Default presets: GlobalSettings_Strategic.rpk. Strategic levels, active bonus-property links, and cost definitions: StrategicAI.rpk. Battle profiles: CoreDefinitions_Tactical.rpk.
  • Editor serialization definitions supplied field names and defaults. The English localization supplied Forgiving, Challenging, and Challenging+ names and descriptions.
  • The comparison uses active links. Older unit-recruitment and upkeep-discount definitions remain in the package without being enabled by some presets.
  • This is static analysis of installed definitions, not a measurement from a running save. Story realms, mods, faction traits, custom settings, and other bonuses can change actual behavior or costs.
  • Manta's income-multiplier removal comes from the official Manta update notes. Other rows remain the installed pre-Manta baseline until an updated binary comparison is available.
