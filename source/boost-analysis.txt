Age of Wonders 4: Infrastructure discounts and building boosts
Checked against installed game data on 25 October 2026.

Result
Infrastructure discounts add together. A building boost is a separate 0.70 multiplier applied to the resulting resource cost. The boost applies once when the linked boost entry's requirements are met.
Nominal remaining cost: (1 - 0.25 * matching infrastructure skills) * 0.70.
Nominal total discount: 30%, 47.5%, 65%, or 82.5% with zero, one, two, or three matching infrastructure skills.
Resource amounts are rounded by the engine, so displayed whole-number costs can differ from the nominal percentages.
