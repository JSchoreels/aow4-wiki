Sources
GlobalSettings_Strategic.rpk: Quick Settings resources select strategic level,
tactical profile, world threat, and random starting-bonus distribution.
StrategicAI.rpk: AI Lord Difficulty Lvl 1 through 4 and their linked properties.
CoreDefinitions_Tactical.rpk: House Easy, Medium, and Hard tactical profiles.
ResourceED.exe: serialization field names, types, defaults, and editor help.
Language/EN/EN.MO: Forgiving, Challenging, and Challenging+ descriptions.
Source-package hashes and decoded records are in ai-difficulty-data.json.

Method and interpretation
Resource names and IDs were read from the local RPK records. Scalar field IDs
were matched to editor serialization definitions. Omitted fields use editor
defaults (including Brutal's one-turn hero cooldown and relation boosting).
Only bonus properties linked by the difficulty records count as enabled.
Legacy Unit Discount and some Unit Upkeep Discount records remain in the
package, but their presence alone does not mean the AI receives the bonus.

Army scaler 0.25/0.50/0.75/1.00 means an army-power target 25/50/75/100 percent
above the strongest human, not free units or guaranteed army strength.
War-turn and CB/justification values are permission gates and adjustments.
The city allowance is permission to exceed the cap, not an increase to it.
General building costs use remaining-cost factors 1/0.85/0.66/0.50 for
Easy/Normal/Hard/Brutal. Town Halls have additional specific modifiers.
Rank rows cover recruited units; summoned units have separate properties.

Tactical profiles
The Medium and Hard profiles have matching non-label decision fields except
their damage modifier block. Hard adds +0.20 damage dealt and -0.20 received.
The English names are Challenging and Challenging+. Easy is Forgiving.
This establishes profile settings; it does not demonstrate deeper planning.

Limits
This is static analysis rather than an observation from a running save.
Story scripts, mods, custom setup, and other faction bonuses can override or
combine with these values. Nominal cost modifiers are before rounding.
